1.0.4

  • Wishlist priority markers and sorting, with clearer photo-list details
  • Improved bulk wine editing and AI prompt copying and result pasting
  • Distinct missing-vintage and NV labels, with refined wine-type icons in same-wine and tasting-history views
  • Improved cellar name, delete controls and shelf layouts, with scrolling and layer-navigation overlap fixes
  • Improved QR wine photo selection and refined print sizes, location labels and filenames
  • Clearer QR generation guidance and confirmation before reissuing codes

1.0.3

  • Improved cellar organization guidance, recommendations and actions for each working method
  • Section-wide unloading confirmation, next-layer navigation in the working direction and selected-wine swaps in full stacked layers
  • Owned counts for same-name wishlist wines and pinned selected quantities and estimated totals
  • Photo reuse when adding wines or tastings to the wishlist and support for entries without a vintage
  • Refined QR output options and sizes, with generation, saving and printing progress indicators
  • Repeated QR print headers, separate cellar, layer and position columns, and planned locations for organization QR printouts
  • Easier overview vintage and drinking-window editing, with empty missing-information sections hidden
  • Disposal confirmation at zero remaining, with storage locations cleared and photos and tasting records preserved
  • Refined bottle-size and remaining-amount entry, read-only values and narrow-screen and large-text layouts

1.0.2

  • Improved home and cellar setup and editing, invalid-cellar recovery and record-preserving cellar deletion
  • Inline wine editing and duplication, with easier name copying and AI-assisted information entry
  • Refined tasting creation, editing and duplication, amounts, ratings, notes and return navigation
  • Inline wishlist editing and purchase completion, with estimated totals in the default currency
  • Monthly tasting calendar, dated records and cellar-specific price ranges and reporting filters
  • Photo zooming and repositioning within a fixed crop frame
  • Improved vintage and drinking-window selection, price calculator, input-row heights, scroll retention and lists

1.0.1

  • Numeric keyboards for numeric-only fields
  • Fix for tapping the top of the screen not scrolling to the top on some screens
  • Easier crop-handle selection and movement during photo editing
  • Search scroll position retained after editing a wine
  • Vertical table-row scrollbars in Search, Tastings and cellar all-layers views
  • Photo rotation angle retained when releasing the slider
  • Complete tasting history when opened from the tasting list, regardless of list filters

1.0.0

Major features

  • Cellar layer and position setup, with bottle location, count and remaining-amount management
  • Wine details, photos, purchase locations, prices, currencies, drinking windows and target tasting dates
  • Multiple tastings per bottle, with amounts, ratings, aromas, photos and notes
  • Search, filtering and sorting in table or photo lists, individually or grouped by wine
  • Combined holdings, purchase and tasting history for the same wine
  • Cellar layout planning and bottle movement and position swapping
  • Collection, tasting and purchase statistics by wine type, country and vintage
  • AI label recognition for wine information entry from photos
  • Tasting-based preference analysis and collection recommendations for taste, food and occasion
  • External-AI prompt copying for analysis and recommendations
  • Separate wine wishlist and registration of purchased wines in the collection
  • Wine, layer and cellar QR labels, with scanning for linked storage information
  • Wine and tasting activity badges and badge-image sharing
  • Wine and tasting CSV import and export, plus file-based backup and restore with photos
  • Language, theme and text-size settings
