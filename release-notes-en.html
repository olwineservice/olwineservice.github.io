한국어 English

1.0.2

  • Improved home and cellar setup and editing, with recovery for invalid cellars and cellar deletion that preserves wine records.
  • Edit and duplicate wines directly from wine details, with easier wine-name copying and AI-assisted information entry.
  • Refined tasting creation, editing and duplication, including amounts, ratings, notes and returning to the previous screen.
  • Edit and complete purchases directly from wishlist details, and view the estimated total in your default currency.
  • View a monthly tasting calendar and records by date, with cellar-specific price ranges and reporting filters.
  • Zoom and move photos while keeping the crop frame fixed.
  • Improved vintage and drinking-window selection, the price calculator, stable input-row heights and scroll positions, and list displays.

1.0.1

  • Numeric-only fields now display a numeric keyboard.
  • Fixed an issue where tapping the top of the screen did not scroll to the top on some screens.
  • Made crop handles easier to select and move when editing photos.
  • Preserved the search scroll position when returning after editing a wine.
  • Added vertical scrollbars within table rows in Search, Tastings, and the all-layers view of cellar details.
  • Improved the photo rotation slider so the angle stays at the selected value when you lift your finger.
  • Opening tasting history from the tasting list now shows all tastings, regardless of the list filters.

1.0.0

Major features

  • Set up cellar layers and positions, and track bottle locations, counts, and remaining amounts.
  • Record wine details, photos, purchase locations, prices, currencies, drinking windows, and target tasting dates.
  • Keep multiple tastings per bottle with amounts, ratings, aromas, photos, and notes.
  • Search, filter, and sort wines in table or photo lists, individually or grouped by wine.
  • View your holdings, purchases, and tasting history for the same wine together.
  • Plan cellar layouts and reorganize bottles by moving or swapping positions.
  • Explore collection, tasting, and purchase statistics by wine type, country, and vintage.
  • Use AI label recognition to help fill in wine information from a photo.
  • Analyze your tasting preferences and get recommendations from your collection for your taste, food, or occasion.
  • Copy prompts for an external AI and use them for analysis and recommendations.
  • Keep a separate wine wishlist and register purchased wines in your collection.
  • Create QR labels for wines, layers, and cellars, and scan them to view linked storage information.
  • Collect badges through wine and tasting activities, and share badge images.
  • Import and export wine and tasting records as CSV, and back up or restore records with photos in a file.
  • Choose your language, theme, and text size.
